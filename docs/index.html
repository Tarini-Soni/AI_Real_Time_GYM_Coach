From camera to feedback in three steps.

  1. 1

    Open the app

    Allow camera access when your browser asks. Nothing to install.

  2. 2

    Get in frame

    Step back until your whole body is visible, then start your set.

  3. 3

    Follow the cues

    The coach counts your reps and flags form problems as they happen.

What it does for you.

Live pose tracking

MediaPipe locates your shoulders, hips, knees and ankles in every frame of your webcam feed.

Automatic rep counting

The coach keeps count, so you can put your attention on the set instead of the number.

Coaching in plain language

An AI model, served through Groq, turns your movement data into short tips you can act on.

Metrics on screen

Rep counts and form metrics update live next to your video as you train.
